FAQ

Questions teams ask first

How much history do we need?

Patterns start to appear with about a dozen hires in the same role. Curial tells you how confident each finding is, and says so when there is not enough data yet.

Does Curial make hiring decisions?

No. It shows evidence about your process. People decide who to hire, and every finding links back to the records behind it.

What about AI-in-hiring regulation?

Curial reviews your process rather than screening individual candidates. We walk through your obligations, such as bias audits, with your legal team during the pilot.

How long does a pilot take?

Most pilots focus on one role and run for six weeks, from connecting the first source to a findings review with your team.
